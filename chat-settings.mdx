## Screenshots

Screenshots can be blocked in chats. Admins will always be notified when a screenshot is taken, regardless of settings.

1. Open the desired chat.
2. Tap the chat’s name at the top of the screen.
3. Scroll to **Screenshots** and tap it.
4. Choose whether to disallow screenshots.

## Who Can Send

Limit who is allowed to send messages in a chat.

1. Open the desired chat.
2. Tap the chat’s name at the top of the screen.
3. Scroll to **Who Can Send** and tap it.
4. Choose either **All Members** or **Only Admins**.

## Adding Others

Control who can add people to a group chat.

1. Open the desired chat.
2. Tap the chat’s name at the top of the screen.
3. Scroll to **Add Others** and tap it.
4. Choose one of:
   1. **All Members**
   2. **Members (Approval Required)**
   3. **Admins Only**

Many other settings can be changed in chats. Please reach out to [help@doorlist.app](mailto:help@doorlist.app) with any questions
