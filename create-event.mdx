---
title: "Create an Event"
description: "Learn how to create an organization on DoorList"
---

1. From the **Home** tab, tap the **\+** icon in the middle of the dock.
   - You can also create an event from your group’s **Admin** page.
2. Tap **Create Event**.
3. Add your event details:
   - Title
   - Host
   - Start time
   - (Optional) Banner image, end time, location, and description
4. Tap **Next** in the upper-right corner.
5. Select who can attend and whether the event will be free or paid.
6. Tap **Create** in the upper-right corner to publish your event.
