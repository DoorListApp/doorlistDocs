---
title: "Connections & Verification"
description: "Connections and Verification allow you to invite other organizations to events"
---

**Connections:** A connection is created by sending a request to the admin of another organization. Once accepted, both groups can invite each other to future events.

**Verification:** Groups can apply for verification after creating a group or by emailing [**help@doorlist.app**](mailto:help@doorlist.app). Verification is designed for social groups that regularly invite others. Verified groups at a campus can freely invite one another to events.

## How to create a connection:

1. Go to the **Organization Admin** page.
2. Tap **Connections** in the header.
3. Share the connection link with the admin of the group you want to connect with.
4. Once accepted, the group will appear in your **Connections** list.

## How do I invite connected/verified organizations?

1. Create an event.
2. Open the **Invite Groups** page.
3. Verified groups can invite other verified groups.
4. Connected groups can view and invite their connections.
