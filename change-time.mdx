---
title: "Change Event Date and Time"
description: "How to change the date and time of an event on DoorList"
---

1. Open the event tile (from the **Home** or **Calendar** tab).
2. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Edit Event**.
4. Choose a new date and time.
5. Tap **Save** in the upper right corner.
