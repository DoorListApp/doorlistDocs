# Who should be an admin?

Admins have full control over your organization. Before adding someone, make sure you trust them with:

- Add or remove organization members
- Add or remove organization admins
- Create new events for your organization
- Add or remove users from your event lists and subgroups
- Trigger payouts  after paid events
- Change chat settings

## Adding Admins:

1. Open the members list by tapping **Members** at the top of the group page.
2. Tap the **\+** in the top right and select **Add Admins**, **OR**
3. Tap an existing member you’d like to promote:
   1. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** in the top-right of the pop-up modal
   2. Select **Make Admin**

## Removing Admins:

1. Open the members list either by tapping on **Members** at the top of the group page
2. Tap the admin you'd like to remove
3. Tap the **⋮ (three dots)** in the top-right of the pop-up modal.
4. Select **Revoke Admin Title**.
