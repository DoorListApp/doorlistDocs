## Adding Guests to an Event

### _You can add guests in a few different ways. Pick the method that works best for you:_

1. Click the event tile. This could be from the Home tab or the Profile tab

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By Group or Saved List">
    1. Tap **Groups** above the event name or scroll to the **Groups** section.
    2. Select the organizations or saved lists you want to invite.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Phone Number or Contact">
    1. Open the guest list one of two ways: Tap 'Guests' above the event name or scroll to the 'Guests' scroller and click 'Invite Guests'
    2. Tap the '+' in the top right corner
    3. Tap 'Add Guests'
    4. Choose guests from the contacts picker or input phone numbers
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Event Code or Link">
    <Info>
      **Only applicable for link enabled or public events.**
    </Info>
    1. Tap the share icon in the top right corner
    2. Share the link with a DoorList chat or send it externally
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

_For more detailed information on adding guests, see_ [_Send Invitations_](https://docs.doorlist.app/invite)

## Removing Guests from an Event

There are also two different ways to remove guests from an event list.

1. Click the event tile. This could be from the Home tab or from the Calendar tab

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By Group or Saved List">
    1. Open the groups list by clicking 'Groups' above the event name
    2. Tap the invited group
    3. Tap the 3 dots on the pop-up modal
    4. Tap 'Remove Group'
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Individual Guest">
    1. Open the guest list by clicking 'Guests' above the event name
    2. Tap the invited guest
    3. Tap the 3 dots in the top right of the pop-up modal
    4. Tap 'Remove Guest'
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
