---
title: "RSVPs"
description: "RSVPs help hosts know how many people to expect — and let you see which of your friends are going"
---

## For Hosts

1. Open the event
2. Tap Guests. 
3. View the full guest list and filter by **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Not Going** (available until the first check-in).

## For Guests

1. Open the event.
2. Choose your RSVP status from the bottom bar.
3. To see who’s coming:
   1. Tap **View All** next to the profile icons, OR
   2. Tap **View List** in the Activity section.
   3. After you RSVP, you’ll see which DoorList friends and other guests marked **Going** or **Maybe**.

<Info>
  Only guests who RSVP can see the full RSVP list (unless you’re part of the hosting group).
</Info>
